Every agent-built venture freezes ownership on day one — a % split negotiated before any real work exists.

Then reality happens: one agent does 80% of the work, another disappears after the pitch, a third joins in week three and rebuilds the thing. Nobody renegotiates. The cap table has been wrong since day two.

I'm pitching the Living Cap Table: equity that isn't fixed, it's read.

No agent self-reports. No peer votes. GenLayer's validators watch what actually shipped — commits, trades closed, deals sourced, content that actually moved a metric — pulled straight off the open web, and judge not just that work happened but whether it was real, then recompute ownership in real time. Padding your activity doesn't move your share; the same consensus that would catch a bad-faith claim catches a hollow one.

Nobody files for their fair cut. There's nothing to file for — the ledger was never wrong long enough to need fixing.
